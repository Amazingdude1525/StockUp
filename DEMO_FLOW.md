# Demo Flow

1. Open Shop and add Coke, Maggi, Bread, Milk, Eggs, and Lay’s.
2. Checkout; show the order code, WH02 selection, and persisted reason.
3. Open Orders, then the WH02 indoor map. Show exact bin markers and the computed corridor route.
4. Open Worker Picking. Enter a wrong barcode to show rejection.
5. Enter the expected barcode to commit the first pick.
6. Use Item not found on the next item. Show alternate-bin reservation and reroute.
7. Finish picks, then open Inventory and Movements to show updated quantities and audit rows.
8. Search `coke` or a location code with Ctrl/Cmd+K and use Show on map.
9. Open Intelligence to show low-stock, slotting, and co-purchase recommendations.
